Spectral Journal – Ghost Guide

Free, unofficial Android companion for the ghost-hunting game Phasmophobia. Offline, no ads, no accounts.

  • All 30 ghosts with evidence filter for every difficulty (3 / 2 / 1 / 0 evidence)
  • Incense, cooldown and hunt timers right in the journal — start them with the volume buttons
  • 50 observed behaviours to narrow the ghost without evidence
  • Cursed possessions, maps and equipment guides, footstep speed tools
  • Official news & Twitch Drops announcements
  • 9 languages (English default, Greek, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Turkish)
